Adds a drag-and-drop timeline view (OCA ``web_timeline``) over the shift
planning lines, grouped by employee: assigned shifts in green,
unassigned in red, leaves in yellow. The visual equivalent of the Odoo
Enterprise Planning Gantt, in Community.
