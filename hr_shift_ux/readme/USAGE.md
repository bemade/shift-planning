1. Open *Shifts > Planning*: each week card shows its state, the
   assigned day shifts, the open slots, the coverage gaps and the
   publication badge.
2. Open a week: follow the numbered buttons — **1. Create the week
   shifts**, **2. Auto-assign employees**, **3. Check coverage**,
   **4. Send schedules to employees**. The alerts on the form always
   tell what remains to be done.
3. The *Employees of the week* tab lists every employee with their
   weekly shift and planned hours; assign the weekly shift right there
   or open the day-by-day detail.
4. When gaps remain, the *Coverage gaps* tab (and the red alert) lists
   them by date with the **Launch cascade** and **Split in two**
   buttons.
