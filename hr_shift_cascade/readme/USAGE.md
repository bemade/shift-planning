1. Check the coverage of a planning (*Check Coverage* button) and open
   the coverage gaps list.
2. Click **Launch Cascade** on a gap: the ordered call list opens.
3. Review the order (drag to reorder if needed) and click **Start**.
4. Call the candidates from top to bottom and log each outcome with the
   **Accepts** / **Declines** / **No answer** buttons.
5. On acceptance the shift is assigned immediately and the remaining
   candidates are skipped. If every candidate declines, the cascade is
   marked **Exhausted** and requires manual intervention.
