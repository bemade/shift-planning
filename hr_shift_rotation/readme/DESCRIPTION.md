Defines rotating week patterns — the staple of 24/7 operations
(alternating weekends, 2-2-3 cycles, week on / week off) — and applies
them over a range of weekly plannings in one action.

A rotation is an ordered cycle of week patterns (each pattern assigns
a shift template per week day). Employees join the rotation with an
offset: on a two-week cycle, offsets 0 and 1 make two employees
alternate. Applying the rotation creates or completes the plannings of
the requested weeks; already assigned shifts are preserved unless the
overwrite option is checked.
