1. Go to *Shifts > Coverage Rules*.
2. Create one rule per shift template and staffing requirement. For
   example, for a "Night" template: one rule requiring 2 employees with
   the "Attendant" job position, and another rule requiring 1 employee
   with the "Auxiliary Nurse" job position.
3. Leave the job position empty to count any assigned employee, and set
   a department to restrict the count to that department.

Different staffing needs on different week days (e.g. weekends) are
expressed with separate shift templates covering the relevant day spans.
