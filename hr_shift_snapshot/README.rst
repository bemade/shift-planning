Every time a shift planning is published, this module stores a frozen
snapshot of the assigned lines (employee, day, shift). The published
schedule can then be compared with the current state of the planning at
any time: a variance report lists every slot that changed after
publication, with its cause when it can be traced back to a shift swap
or a replacement cascade — anything else is flagged as a manual change.

Changes made to assigned shifts after publication are also logged in the
planning chatter, so the full history of "planned versus worked" is
auditable.

Usage
=====

1. Publish a planning as usual (*Shifts > Planning*, button
   **Publish**). A snapshot version is stored automatically.
2. Open *Shifts > Publications* (or the **Publications** smart button on
   the planning) and pick a version.
3. Press **Analyse Variances** to rebuild the divergence list between
   that published schedule and the planning as it stands now. Manual
   changes are highlighted; swaps and cascades are named.
4. Post-publication edits are also logged in the planning chatter as
   they happen.
