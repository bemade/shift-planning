As an employee:

1. Declare your availabilities in *Shifts > My Availabilities*.
2. Claim an open slot from the coverage gaps list (*Claim* button), or
   offer one of your shifts in *Shifts > Shift Swaps*.
3. When a colleague offers you a swap, you are notified and can accept
   or refuse it from the request.

As a shift manager:

1. Review pending claims in *Shifts > Open Shift Claims* (filter *To
   Approve*) and pending swaps in *Shifts > Shift Swaps*.
2. Approving a claim assigns the employee to the shift; approving a
   swap updates both shift lines. Coverage gaps are recomputed
   automatically.
